Ask on WhatsApp
Is the theatre fully private?

Yes. The whole hall is only for your group during your slot.

Do you provide movies?

No. Bring your own OTT login (Netflix, Prime, Hotstar and others), a pen drive or your phone. We connect it for you.

How many people can come?

Packages include up to 4 guests. You can add more, up to 10, at ₹100 per extra guest.

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes, you can bring your own cake. Or choose a package that includes one.

Can we bring food and snacks?

Yes, simple snacks and soft drinks are fine. Please keep the hall clean.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel 24 hours before for a full refund or a new date. Later cancellations are not refunded.

Can we play our own photos and videos?

Yes. Send them on WhatsApp before your slot and we will play them on the big screen.
